MOSSBOUND ARENA — V2 ART DIRECTION

Requested direction: Cute/Minimal contrasted with Dark Fantasy / Melancholie.
Appearance reference: mossbound-arena-v2-melancholy.png
Multi-view appearance reference: mossbound-arena-v2-reference-sheet.png
Geometry authority remains mossbound-arena-plan-v1.svg and mossbound-arena-reference-spec.txt.

Use large softly bevelled architectural masses, broad matte stone surfaces,
few floor seams, simple timber construction and sparse broad moss patches.
Reduce tiny bricks, ornamental detail, texture noise and dense foliage.
Palette: charcoal, slate blue, fog grey, desaturated sage, muted burgundy.
Small amber eyes and the hero's rusty red scarf provide emotional warmth.
Preserve the existing cute moss hero and solemn bronze knight identities.
Atmosphere: pale overcast light, bare branches, layered ravine mist, quiet loss.
Keep combat floor and silhouettes clearly readable in the cool lighting.

The illustrated overhead is an elevated appearance view, not a measured plan.
The sheet's east path looks raised; build it at Z=0 per measured specification.
Illustrations do not supersede dimension targets or traversal requirements.
This revision changes visual references only; no Unreal level has been modified.
