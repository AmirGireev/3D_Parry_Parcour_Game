MOSSBOUND FORT — ARENA REFERENCE SPECIFICATION v1

Purpose: proposed visual design for a future Unreal environment build.
This is not a survey of the existing L_MossboundFort blockout.
Generated views communicate appearance; this written layout governs dimensions.
Check all dimensions with the existing characters and movement before final art.

COORDINATES
Arena centre = (0,0,0). All measurements below are metres.
North = Unreal +X; east = Unreal +Y; Z = height. Unreal units = metres * 100.
On the plan north is up, east right. Court floor Z = 0.

LAYOUT
Court: circular, 20 m outer diameter; 14 m diameter clear central combat floor.
Outer 3 m band: architecture, circulation and planting only.
Gatehouse: north / 12 o'clock; 6 m wide, 3 m deep, roughly 7 m high.
Gate opening: 3 m wide, 4.5 m high; burgundy banner above.
West balcony: 8–11 o'clock, 2.5 m wide, deck Z = +2 m.
Balcony stairs: southwest / 8 o'clock; ascend northwards; 1.5 m wide.
Stairs: provisional 12 risers at 0.1667 m, 0.30 m tread, roughly 3.3 m run.
East timber walkway: 2–4 o'clock, 1.5 m wide, court level.
Entry bridge: southeast / 5 o'clock, 3 m wide, 8 m long, court level.
Southwest: rooted tree and broken stone arch at 7 o'clock.
Hero reference height: 1 m. Fallen knight reference height: 3 m.
Ravine: visually below platform; depth and collision require blockout decisions.

ART
Warm grey chunky stone; weathered dark timber; olive/moss green growth.
Muted burgundy banners connect architecture with the knight's cape.
Soft warm afternoon sunlight; cool forest shade; mist only beyond the arena.
Radial paving motif remains flush; moss mainly in perimeter and narrow seams.
Keep floor, sword silhouettes, approach routes and edge conditions readable.

BUILD USE
Use the top plan for footprint and landmark placement.
Use elevations for height relationships and modular architecture.
Use perspectives for material, lighting, foliage and visual composition.
Resolve any generated-view disagreement against this specification and the plan.
Do not infer reliable collision or walkability from an illustration.
Validate camera clearance, knight swings, dash distance, stairs and fall edges in Unreal.
No gameplay, level assets or existing project source changed for this reference set.
